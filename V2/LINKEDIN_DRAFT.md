**Day 13 of #30DayMapChallenge: Interactions. Follow the tea.**

Yesterday (Day 12, Power) I mapped how much energy goes into growing and drying the world's coffee and tea. Today the tea leaves the factory.

The theme is Interactions, so I made the map itself interactive. It shows every recorded tea export from the world's five biggest exporters (Kenya, China, India, Sri Lanka and Vietnam) to 170+ countries: 580 trade corridors in all.

Click any country to see who it trades with:
- **Pakistan** gets 84% of its tea from Kenya: 221,000 tonnes a year, the single biggest tea corridor in the world.
- **Morocco** buys almost all of its tea from China. It is green gunpowder tea for mint tea, and the same trail runs on to Mauritania, Senegal, The Gambia and Ghana.
- **Iraq** splits its cup between India and Sri Lanka. Iraq is Sri Lanka's biggest market, ahead of Russia.
- **The UK** still drinks mostly Kenyan tea: 65,000 tonnes.
- **Kenya** sells to 100 countries, but more than a third of everything goes to Pakistan.

Each producer has its own sphere of influence, shaped less by distance than by history and habit. Kenya looks west and north to Pakistan, Egypt and the UK. China supplies North and West Africa. Sri Lanka and India supply the Gulf, Iran, Iraq and Russia.

Built with Python (pandas, GeoPandas, the UN Comtrade API) and deck.gl, in plain lat/long.
